Delete Your Verdantoff Account

If you wish to permanently delete your Verdantoff account and associated data, you may do so directly within the Verdantoff mobile application.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Verdantoff app.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm your request.

What Happens After Deletion

  • Your account profile information will be permanently deleted.
  • Access to your account will be removed immediately.
  • Call content is not stored and therefore is not retained.
  • Technical logs may be retained temporarily for security and fraud prevention purposes, as described in our Privacy Policy.
