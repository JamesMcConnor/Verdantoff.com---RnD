Help & Support – Verdantoff

Welcome to Verdantoff. We are committed to providing a secure and reliable communication platform. This page explains how to get help, report issues, and understand your rights as a user.

1. Safety & Community Standards

Verdantoff enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy against harmful content.

  • Child exploitation or abuse
  • Hate speech or incitement to violence
  • Harassment, threats, or bullying
  • Non-consensual intimate imagery or extreme violence
  • Illegal activities (e.g., drugs, trafficking, terrorism)

Enforcement: Violations may result in immediate suspension or permanent account termination. Serious cases may be reported to law enforcement.

2. Reporting & User Protection

2.1 Report Content or Users

  • Use the in-app Report function
  • Email: jadconnor@gmail.com (Subject: Safety Report)

2.2 Block Users

You can block any user via their profile or chat settings. Blocked users cannot contact you.

2.3 Moderation Timeline

All reports are reviewed within 24 hours.

3. Technical Support

If you experience issues:

  • Ensure your app is updated
  • Restart the app/device

When contacting support, include:

  • Device type (Android / iPhone)
  • App version
  • Brief description of the issue

4. Account & Data

4.1 Delete Your Account

You may permanently delete your account at any time.

  • In-app: Profile → Settings → Delete Account
  • Email: jadconnor@gmail.com

Deletion removes your profile and associated data from active systems.

4.2 Privacy

See our Privacy Policy for full details on data handling.

5. Contact

  • Email: jadconnor@gmail.com
  • Response time: 48–72 hours
